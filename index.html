Who We Serve

We do not believe in one-size-fits-all legal defense. Our practice is centered around resolving distinct human crises across three focus groups.

The Wrongfully Accused

You are being defined by something you did not do. You are a Victim of malicious or fabricated accusations, lying accusers, mistaken eyewitness identification, flawed police investigations, or junk science. Our California criminal lawyers conduct a thorough, independent investigation to expose inconsistencies, challenge unreliable testimony, uncover hidden motives, and highlight the factual gaps necessary to build a defense and prove your innocence.

Rights Violated

When law enforcement oversteps, your constitutional rights deserve fierce protection. We represent individuals who have been subjected to illegal traffic stops, unlawful arrests, unconstitutional searches, or vindindicative prosecutions. By rigorously challenging constitutional violations, we work to suppress improperly obtained evidence and hold the system accountable.

The Human in Crisis

We defend good people facing difficult circumstances, including those struggling with untreated trauma, drug addiction, alcoholism, or severe mental health conditions. Our California criminal defense team advocates for treatment-focused solutions and alternative resolutions whenever possible, helping clients move forward without being defined by a conviction. We treat you with empathy, not judgment.

Our Methodology

The Pernik Three-Step Method

Identifying the factual or clinical root causes behind your crisis to restore your future.

Step One

Identify the Crisis

We ask the "Why" that the system ignores.

We look beyond the surface of a police report to find the engine driving the crisis.

The Factual Why

Our South Bay and Central Coast specialist investigates witness bias, detective tunnel vision, and fundamental factual errors. We challenge unreliable testimony by exposing hidden motives early.

The Clinical Why

Up to 75% of cases involve untreated trauma, neurodivergence, or mental health breakdowns. We pinpoint these as the true root causes behind your crisis to build a customized defense.

Step Two

Solve the Cause

Building a Narrative of Restoration.

We dismantle the government’s architecture to craft a superior, evidence-based narrative.

1. In-House Investigation: Most California law firms outsource their investigation work. At Pernik Law, we work with our full-time in-house investigator to look beyond the police report to find the critical factual gaps and biases that the state missed.

2. Expert Network: We collaborate with an established network of mental health experts, addiction specialists, and trauma clinicians to create an individualized treatment-based action plan.

3. Path of Restoration: We don't just solve the legal problem; we close the cycle of crisis by leading you on a path of clinical restoration and long-term life stability.

Step Three

Advocate for Results

Narrative Dominance in the Courtroom.

We place this professional solution at the dead center of our advocacy - forcing prosecutors and judges to view you as a human being, whether at trial, during negotiations, or inside specialized diversion programs.

Court Humanization

Shifting courtroom perception by presenting your complete factual and clinical background.

Specialty Diversion

Securing formal Mental Health (PC 1001.36) or Military Diversion to dismiss charges entirely.

Professional Shield

Safeguarding medical, legal, and commercial licenses from state disciplinary boards.

Immigration Defense

Mitigating deportation risks by tailoring all plea agreements to satisfy federal immigration law.

We use the clinical solutions and investigative work from previous steps to show the court the human that you are, advocating for recovery over conviction.
